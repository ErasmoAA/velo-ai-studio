# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Velo AI Studio is an open-source project intended to be welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

We are committed to providing a respectful environment where people can collaborate constructively.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions from people who are learning the project.
- Respect different viewpoints and technical approaches.
- Keep issue reports, pull requests, and community discussions focused on improving Velo.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information about others are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or close comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent loss of participation privileges.

## Reporting

For security vulnerabilities, follow `SECURITY.md` and do not disclose sensitive details publicly.

For other conduct concerns, open a private communication with the project maintainer through the contact information available on the repository profile.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and interactions where a contributor is representing or participating in the Velo AI Studio community.
